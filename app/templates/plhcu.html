How to Understand This Chart

This chart explains why the AI system arrived at this referral recommendation by illustrating how each bedside clinical finding impacts the mother's risk:

Red Bars (Danger Signs):

These clinical parameters (such as elevated blood pressure, abnormal fetal heart rate/bradycardia, or severe anemia) extend rightwards from the center baseline and push towards emergency referral to a higher facility (SDH/DH). The longer the red bar, the higher the danger.

Blue Bars (Safe / Protective Signs):

These reassuring parameters (such as normal fetal heart rate ~140 bpm, stable maternal vitals, or clear liquor) extend leftwards from the center baseline, confirming it is safe to proceed with normal labor progression and delivery at the local primary health center.

Center Baseline:

The prominent vertical reference line down the center of each bar represents the average baseline risk E[f(X)] = 0.49 (zero net delta). The AI balances these opposing red and blue clinical factors to determine the final Green, Yellow, or Red triage decision.
